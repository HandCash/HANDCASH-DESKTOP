The action bar reads as one bordered object: selected section + label share the opaque obsidian `--hc-select-3d` plate with square corners (no translucent white stops or white inset box-shadows, which Blitz paints gray); unselected tabs hold a distinct flat matte plate.
Dropdown panels stay black with `--hc-select-3d` square-corner selected rows, and Send/Swap/Receive use the same square `--hc-select-3d` plate only when selected/pressed.
