name: restricted-code-replacement
when: replacing BSV-restricted components or making licensing claims
---
Meridian targets first-principles replacement of BSVA implementation and BSV-restricted code, with a scoped internal request language behind BRC-100 and separately reviewed standards adapters; exposed contributors must not claim clean-room provenance or copy/translate legacy code.
Use permission-checked specs/vectors and permissive crypto; retain notices until source, transitive dependencies and artifacts are cleared, with independent/legal review for high-risk work and the user's license choice for verified-original code.
